About Zorevo

Zorevo is an India-focused cashback and rewards platform, based in New Delhi. We help shoppers get part of their money back when they buy from partner stores online.

What we do

A simple idea, done transparently

Online stores pay a referral commission when someone arrives from a partner site and makes a purchase. Zorevo is that partner site. When you shop through a Zorevo link, we share part of the commission with you as cashback.

We started Zorevo because cashback often feels confusing: unclear rates, rewards that disappear and terms that are hard to find. We want every shopper in India to know exactly what they can expect before they buy.

How Zorevo earns money

Zorevo's revenue comes from commissions paid by partner stores for eligible sales. We don't charge shoppers any fee to join, shop or withdraw cashback, and we don't sell your personal data.

Because we're paid only when a store confirms a sale, we pay cashback only on confirmed purchases.

Our principles

How we work

Clear terms

Rates, exclusions and timelines are shown on the offer, not buried in fine print.

No false promises

We never promise income or guaranteed returns. Cashback is a reward on eligible spending.

Respect for data

We collect what's needed to track and pay rewards, and protect it in line with Indian law.

Real support

Every message gets a reply from a person on our team within 2 business days.
