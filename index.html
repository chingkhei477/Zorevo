How it works

Three steps, no guesswork

Use Zorevo as the starting point for shopping you were going to do anyway.

  1. Pick an offer

    Choose a partner store and read its cashback rate, eligible categories and conditions before you leave Zorevo.

  2. Shop as usual

    Our link takes you to the store. Complete your purchase in the same visit so it can be tracked to you.

  3. Get your cashback

    Once the store confirms the order after its return period, your cashback is ready to withdraw to UPI or your bank account.

Reward lifecycle

From purchase to payout

Timelines vary by store and are shown on each offer.

  1. Tracked

    The store reports your purchase to us, usually within 72 hours.

  2. Pending

    We wait while the store's return and cancellation window runs.

  3. Confirmed

    The store approves the order and pays its commission.

  4. Paid

    You withdraw your confirmed balance to UPI or your bank.

Good to know

Cashback is a reward, not income

Cashback gives you back part of what you spend. It isn't a way to make money, and it isn't paid on every purchase.

Whether a purchase qualifies is decided by the offer's terms and the store's confirmation. Read our Cashback & Rewards Terms for the full rules.

A purchase may not earn cashback if

  • it is returned, cancelled or exchanged
  • you use a coupon not listed on the Zorevo offer
  • the product category is excluded by the store
  • you didn't complete the purchase in the same visit, or cookies were blocked
  • the store doesn't confirm the order
FAQ

Common questions

Does Zorevo cost anything?

No. Zorevo is free to use. We are paid by partner stores, not by you.

How long until I can withdraw cashback?

Cashback becomes withdrawable once the store confirms your order, which usually happens after its return window ends. For most stores this is between 30 and 90 days. The expected time is shown on each offer.

Will I pay more by shopping through Zorevo?

No. The price you pay is set by the store and is the same as going to the store directly.

What if my cashback doesn't show up?

If a purchase hasn't tracked within 72 hours, contact us with your order details within 30 days of purchase and we'll raise it with the store.
