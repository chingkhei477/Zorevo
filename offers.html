How to read a Zorevo offer

Every store offer includes these details. Check them before you click through.

1Cashback rate

Shown as a percentage of the eligible order value or a fixed amount. Rates can differ between product categories and between new and existing customers.

2Eligible purchases

The products, categories, platforms (website or app) and payment methods that qualify.

3Exclusions

What won't earn cashback, such as specific brands, gift cards, or coupons not listed on the offer.

4Tracking time

How long the store usually takes to report your purchase to Zorevo.

5Confirmation time

The typical period before the store confirms your order and cashback becomes withdrawable.

Offer rules that always apply

  • The price you pay is set by the store and is the same as shopping directly
  • Offers can be changed or ended by the store at any time
  • The rate that applies is the one shown when you made the purchase
  • Cashback is paid only after the store confirms the order

Eligibility depends on offer terms

Not every purchase earns cashback. Eligibility is set by each offer's terms and our Cashback & Rewards Terms. If you're unsure whether a purchase qualifies, ask us before you buy.
