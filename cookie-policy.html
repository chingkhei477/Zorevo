This Cookie Policy explains how Zorevo uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and tracking links) on our website, and how you can control them.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. Similar technologies, such as browser local storage, work in a comparable way. They help websites work, remember your choices and understand how they are used.

2. Cookies we use

Types of cookies used by Zorevo
TypePurposeConsent
EssentialRun the website securely and remember your cookie choice (stored as zorevo_cookie_consent in your browser).Always on
Affiliate trackingSet by partner stores or affiliate networks when you click a Zorevo offer link, so your purchase can be credited to you. Without these, cashback can't be tracked.Set on the store's site when you choose to click through
AnalyticsHelp us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it.Only if you select "Accept all"
AdvertisingMeasure the performance of our ads on platforms such as social media, and show relevant Zorevo ads.Only if you select "Accept all"

3. Your choices

When you first visit, you can choose "Accept all" or "Essential only". You can change your choice at any time:

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking affiliate tracking cookies will stop cashback from tracking on your purchases.

4. Third-party cookies

Partner stores, affiliate networks and advertising platforms may set their own cookies, governed by their own privacy and cookie policies. We don't control these cookies.

5. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy if the cookies we use change. The updated version will be posted on this page. For more on how we use personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
